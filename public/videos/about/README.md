# About Me Video Upload Instructions

## 📁 Upload Your About Me Video Here

Place your about me video in this folder with this exact name:

**`about-me.mp4`**

## 📋 Video Requirements

### **Content Ideas:**
- **Personal Introduction**: Who you are, your passion for cinematography
- **Behind-the-Scenes**: You working with cameras, editing, on set
- **Your Story**: How you got into cinematography/video editing
- **Equipment Showcase**: Your gear, setup, workspace
- **Process Demo**: Quick editing or filming process

### **Technical Requirements:**
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (horizontal/landscape)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD) recommended
- **Duration**: 30-90 seconds (optimal for web)
- **File Size**: Keep under 15MB for good performance
- **Audio**: Include audio if you're speaking (optional)

### **Style Suggestions:**
- **Professional but Personal**: Show your personality
- **Good Lighting**: Well-lit, clear footage
- **Stable Footage**: Use tripod or stabilizer
- **Clear Audio**: If speaking, ensure good audio quality
- **Engaging**: Keep it dynamic and interesting

## 🎬 Video Ideas:

### **Option 1: Talking Head Style**
- You speaking directly to camera
- Introduce yourself and your work
- Professional but friendly tone

### **Option 2: Behind-the-Scenes**
- Montage of you working
- Editing, filming, setting up shots
- Voice-over or music

### **Option 3: Equipment/Workspace Tour**
- Show your setup, cameras, editing station
- Quick tour of your creative space
- Demonstrates professionalism

### **Option 4: Process Demo**
- Quick editing process
- Before/after shots
- Shows your skills in action
