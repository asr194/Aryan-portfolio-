Place your downloaded Instagram reel videos in this folder with these exact names:

- `reel-1.mp4` (for your first Instagram reel)
- `reel-2.mp4` (for your second Instagram reel)  
- `reel-3.mp4` (for your third Instagram reel)
- `reel-4.mp4` (for your fourth Instagram reel)
- `reel-5.mp4` (for your fifth Instagram reel)

## 📋 File Requirements

- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (vertical, like Instagram reels)
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920 recommended
- **File Size**: Keep under 10MB each for best performance
- **Duration**: 15-60 seconds (typical Instagram reel length)

## 🎬 How to Download Your Instagram Reels

1. **Go to your Instagram reel**
2. **Copy the link** (share → copy link)
3. **Use a downloader tool**:
   - [SnapInsta](https://snapinsta.app/) - Free, easy to use
   - [SaveInsta](https://saveinsta.app/) - No registration needed
   - [4K Video Downloader](https://www.4kdownload.com/) - Desktop app
